Quality Rated Star 3 Inspection Quick Checklist

Key compliance directives required by Georgia Department of Early Care and Learning (DECAL) and Environment Rating Scale assessors during unannounced classroom audits.

Strive for 5 Exchanges

Teachers must engage in back-and-forth verbal conversations of at least 5 communicative turns per child. Ask open-ended questions that stimulate reasoning.

20-Second Handwashing

Staff and children must lather hands with soap for at least 20 seconds under warm running water upon entering, before meals, and immediately after diapering.

2-Step Diaper Sanitizing

Spray changing table with soapy water and wipe dry; then spray bleach-water sanitizing solution and allow to air dry for a minimum contact time of 2 minutes.

ECERS Item 28 Diversity

Every classroom must prominently display at least 4 different cultural backgrounds in books, dramatic play props, visual posters, and puzzles reflecting varied abilities.
